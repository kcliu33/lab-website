Research

Digital Biomarkers

Developing objective and clinically meaningful measures of health and physical function from real-world sensor data.

02

Wearable Sensing

Using wearable and mobile sensors to capture movement, physiological signals, and everyday human behavior.

03

Human Movement & Mobility

Quantifying gait, mobility, and physical function across laboratory, clinical, and real-world environments.

AI for Health

OUR APPROACH

From sensing to analytics to clinical insight, we study how everyday data can reveal meaningful changes in human health.

Real-world mobility and physical function

Digital measures of healthy aging

Connecting real-world sensor measurements with mobility, function, and clinically meaningful health outcomes.
